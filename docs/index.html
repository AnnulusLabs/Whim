whim

Tools for people who build their own ideas.

From AnnulusLabs. Everything here runs on your own device and keeps working with no network.

Spatial Bench Build parts by hand, in a headset or on a screen. Fuse shapes, cut holes, clamp it, load it, and see whether it holds on Earth, Mars or the Moon. Export 3MF or STL.

The Whim notebook, calculations with units forked from EngineeringPaper.xyz, lives in the same repository and runs from your own server. Source on GitHub.
