How it works

On a screen

Tap a shape to pick it. Drag it to slide it along the bench. Turn on Lift, or hold Shift, and drag to raise it.

Drag a blue dot to stretch the shape from that side. The opposite side stays where it is.

Drag empty space to look around. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Two fingers or right-drag to pan.

Building

Push shapes together, pick one, and press Fuse touching. Everything in contact becomes one part.

Make a shape a hole, push it into a part, and fuse. The hole is cut out. Break apart gives the shapes back.

Testing

Put a Clamp where the part is held and a Weight where it is loaded. The arrow on the weight shows which way it pushes. Then press Test.

Colour shows stress and red is the peak. Safety factor is the material's strength divided by the peak stress. The part's own weight counts, at the gravity you chose.

The test is a quick straight-line elastic estimate on a grid of bricks, one uniform material, small bending. It leaves out buckling, fatigue, impact, weak print layers, and the extra stress at sharp inside corners. Material figures are typical handbook values. Use the datasheet for your own stock when it matters.

In a headset

This is a preview inside another page. The headset view, offline use and the exact shape engine are off here. They work from the full page, hosted on its own address.

Open this page in the Quest browser and press Enter headset. You see your room with the bench in front of you.

Point at a shape on the shelf along the back and pull the trigger, or pinch. It appears in your hand. Let go to put it down.

The trigger, or a pinch, carries a part and keeps it square. The grip button carries and turns it. While holding a part, press with the other hand and pull your hands apart to stretch it.

The buttons sit on the sloped panel along the front edge. Point and press, or push one with a fingertip. Let go of a part over the red bin to delete it. Grab the grey knob to move, turn or raise the bench.

Files

Export 3MF or STL for a slicer, in millimetres, bench top as the print bed. Save bench file keeps the editable version. Your work stays on this device.
